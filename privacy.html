Privacy policy

Effective November 15, 2026

This policy covers Netflix-AutoClick, a personal Household email watcher operated by its owner.

Google account data

The watcher requests Gmail read-only permission. This permission allows mailbox reading, while the application searches for Netflix Household request and confirmation messages. It reads matching messages, including their headers, timestamps, contents, and confirmation links, to authenticate and process Household changes.

Use and sharing

Message data is processed on the owner's device for the requested automation. Eligible confirmation links are sent to Netflix to perform the Household update. When phone notifications are enabled, brief status messages are sent to ntfy.sh, whose service may cache them for delivery. These notifications exclude email contents, email addresses, and confirmation links.

Google account data is not sold, used for advertising, or used to train AI models. The application's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Storage and retention

Google authorization credentials are stored on the owner's device to maintain access. The watcher stores hashes of attempted confirmation links and attempt timestamps locally to prevent duplicate actions. Entries older than 24 hours are removed when a later link is claimed. The application does not intentionally archive complete emails. Operational status logs may be retained by the device's logging service.

Control and deletion

The owner can stop the watcher and revoke its access through Google Account third-party connections. Local credentials, the link-attempt database, and operational logs can be deleted on the device. Revoking access does not itself delete those local files.

This website and contact

This informational site uses GitHub Pages hosting and includes no application analytics or tracking scripts. GitHub may process technical request information under its own privacy policy.

For questions or deletion assistance, contact the operator through GitHub issues. Please do not post credentials, email contents, or other private information in a public issue.
